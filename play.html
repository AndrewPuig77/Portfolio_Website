Rules

  • Every racer leaves a light trail that lasts about 3 seconds.
  • Hit any trail (even your own) or the wall and you crash.
  • Outlast every CPU to clear the round. Each round is faster.
  • After 15 seconds the arena starts closing in. The edge flashes before it moves.

Pickups

  • Extend: your trail lasts 2 seconds longer for a while.
  • Shrink: everyone else's trails are cut in half.
  • Charge: your boost is ready instantly.

Scoring

  • 10 points for every second you survive.
  • 100 points each time a CPU crashes while you're still riding.
  • 250 bonus points for clearing a round.
  • Your best score for each difficulty is saved in this browser.
